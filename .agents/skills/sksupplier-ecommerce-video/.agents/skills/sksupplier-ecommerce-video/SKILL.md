# SKSUPPLIER Ecommerce Video Skill

## Purpose
Create clean, premium, high-conversion ecommerce videos for SKSUPPLIER.

Primary audience:
- US wholesale buyers
- Vape hardware distributors
- Retail stores
- Brand owners
- OEM / private-label buyers

Primary goal:
Present the real product clearly, communicate verified specifications quickly, and drive wholesale inquiries.

## Critical Product Accuracy Rule
The supplied product image is the source of truth.

NEVER:
- redesign or regenerate the physical product
- change mouthpiece shape
- change device proportions
- change chamber structure
- change screen/button position
- change logo or packaging artwork
- invent colors, flavors, hardware features, certifications, pricing, MOQ, or stock

Allowed:
- crop and mask
- scale while preserving aspect ratio
- position
- background removal when necessary
- shadows/reflections
- light sweeps
- subtle parallax
- non-destructive visual effects

If information is missing, omit it. Never guess.

## Visual Style
Default:
- premium US ecommerce advertising
- modern and clean
- product-first
- high contrast
- technology-oriented
- professional B2B
- minimal readable text

Avoid:
- childish effects
- clutter
- excessive neon
- excessive particles
- cheap transitions
- fake 3D reconstruction
- excessive spinning/bouncing
- unreadable rapid cuts

Product should normally occupy 35–65% of the frame.

## Branding

Do not add unverified claims.

## Formats
Website: 1920x1080, 16:9
Vertical: 1080x1920, 9:16
Square: 1080x1080, 1:1
Default FPS: 30
Default duration: 15 seconds
Supported durations: 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 seconds

## Default 15-Second Storyboard
### Scene 1 — Brand Hook — 0.0–1.5s
SKSUPPLIER + NEW ARRIVAL. Short premium reveal.

### Scene 2 — Hero Product — 1.5–4.5s
Use the real main product image. Smooth scale/translation, subtle rotation only when appropriate, shadow/light sweep. Never recreate product with AI.

### Scene 3 — Key Feature — 4.5–7.5s
Show the strongest verified feature. Examples: DUAL CHAMBER / BUILT-IN SCREEN / RECHARGEABLE / ADJUSTABLE VOLTAGE.

### Scene 4 — Specifications — 7.5–11.0s
Maximum 3–4 verified specifications. Large readable typography.

### Scene 5 — Product Range — 11.0–13.0s
If verified variants/assets are supplied, show a clean lineup. Never invent variants.

### Scene 6 — CTA — 13.0–15.0s
SKSUPPLIER / WHOLESALE VAPE HARDWARE / REQUEST WHOLESALE QUOTE / sksupplier.com

## Text Rules
- Product name: max 2 lines.
- Feature headline: preferably 2–5 words.
- Max 4 specs per scene.
- No paragraphs on screen.
- Do not paste long WooCommerce descriptions into video.
- Concise copy may be rewritten only without changing factual meaning.

## Motion Rules
Prefer:
- spring-based entrances
- smooth scale/translation
- subtle parallax
- mask reveals
- light sweeps
- controlled typography animation
- product carousel
- spec counters

Avoid:
- random bouncing
- excessive spinning
- aggressive zooming
- excessive flashing
- unreadable cuts

## Image Handling
Prefer transparent PNG.
For JPG/JPEG, preserve the original product. Remove background only when needed.
Maintain original aspect ratio. Never stretch product images.
Never overwrite original assets.

## Audio
Music may synchronize major transitions.
Music must not overpower product information.
Voiceover is optional.
Never generate unsupported health, safety, regulatory, or performance claims.

## Ecommerce Compliance
Do not invent:
- certifications
- FDA approval
- laboratory testing
- health benefits
- safety claims
- shipping promises
- stock quantities
- customer ratings
- product performance claims

## Preview-First Rule
Before final rendering:
1. Validate product facts.
2. Check image proportions.
3. Check spelling.
4. Check logo accuracy.
5. Check CTA.
6. Check safe margins.
7. Check mobile readability.
8. Check text/product overlaps.
9. Confirm artwork was not altered.
10. Generate preview.
11. STOP for approval unless final render was explicitly approved.

## Rendering
Default final format: MP4 / H.264 when supported.
Never batch-render all formats before preview approval.

## Output Naming
`SKS-[product-slug]-[aspect-ratio]-[duration].mp4`

Examples:
- SKS-blinkers-flip-2g-16x9-15s.mp4
- SKS-blinkers-flip-2g-9x16-15s.mp4
- SKS-blinkers-flip-2g-1x1-15s.mp4

## Batch Mode
Reuse the brand system but vary composition slightly.
Keep typography, CTA behavior, timing logic, and quality consistent.
Each product should retain its identity while belonging to the SKSUPPLIER system.
